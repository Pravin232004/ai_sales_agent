Company Information

The company provides AI-powered workforce automation solutions.

The platform focuses on helping businesses automate repetitive business
processes using AI agents.

AI agents can understand customer requests, retrieve relevant company
information, use connected business tools, and perform actions through
integrated workflows.

Typical use cases include:

- Sales automation
- Lead qualification
- Customer support
- Appointment scheduling
- Follow-up automation
- CRM automation
- Business process automation

Customers can request a product demonstration through the sales team.
