AI Sales Workforce Platform

Our AI Sales Workforce platform helps businesses automate repetitive sales
and customer engagement tasks using AI agents.

Core capabilities:

- AI-powered customer conversations
- Lead qualification
- Lead capture
- Automated follow-ups
- Demo scheduling
- Customer question answering
- CRM integration
- Email automation
- Workflow automation
- RAG-based company knowledge retrieval

The platform can operate across multiple customer communication channels
and can connect AI agents to business tools and workflows.
